# Onboarding and Support Ticket Boundary

**Independently authored demo explanation; not operational approval.**

An onboarding project tracks confirmed progress; a support ticket tracks a scoped operational issue. Vendor contact membership alone is not project identity. Untagged operational mail does not establish onboarding progress or justify reopening a completed project. At valid M8 completion, residual work is represented by linked support tickets without milestone regression. Preserve evidence, deduplication, explicit scope and operator review. Do not infer a ticket was created from an intention or recommendation.

The executable policy owner is the versioned milestone contract. See [[concept/milestone_state_machine]].
