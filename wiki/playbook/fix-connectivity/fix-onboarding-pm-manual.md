# Demo PM Workflow Overview

**Independently authored demo explanation; not operational approval.**

This is a newly authored overview, not a renamed institutional manual. Establish verified project identity and current scope; inspect current evidence; apply the contract criterion by criterion; stage through authorized tools; confirm through the required review path; distinguish proposed changes from committed results. No market-specific checklist, venue rules, production endpoints, provisioning procedure or real participant roster is bundled. Obtain separately approved local instructions before any operational use.

The executable policy owner is the versioned milestone contract. See [[concept/milestone_state_machine]].
