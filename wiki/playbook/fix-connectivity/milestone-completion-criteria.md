# Milestone Criteria Reading Guide

**Independently authored demo explanation; not operational approval.**

Read required predicates, accepted evidence levels and named-authority requirements directly from milestone-contracts.json. Current bounded evidence is required; planned, conditional, quoted-history and unverified statements are insufficient. A stronger or newer contradiction requires review or abstention. Mandatory conformance exemptions and production-session delivery are reserved to Demo Provisioning Support. PCT scope exemptions and completion may use either support role. The M5 summary/criterion threshold inconsistency remains explicitly unresolved.

The executable policy owner is the versioned milestone contract. See [[concept/milestone_state_machine]].
