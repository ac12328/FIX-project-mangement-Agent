# Completion Evidence Review

**Independently authored demo explanation; not operational approval.**

Review project identity, current source eligibility, the designated support role, a bounded completion quote and any newer contradictory evidence. Scope exemptions require a per-step reason, source and approved authority; blanket exemption of all PCT steps is forbidden. Keep PCT test execution separate from the project's completion decision. This explanatory checklist adds no M8 gates. Audit transactional status and outbox results without claiming that a scheduled or uncertain send succeeded.

The executable policy owner is the versioned milestone contract. See [[concept/milestone_state_machine]].
