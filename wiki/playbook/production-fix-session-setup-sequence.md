# Production Preparation Boundary

**Independently authored demo explanation; not operational approval.**

This edition supplies no institutional production provisioning sequence or filled configuration. Use separately approved local instructions for endpoints, credentials, certificates and TLS order. Preserve source eligibility, authorization, peer/environment validation and evidence retention. Treat delivery of production-session information as distinct from a plan or a client request. Demo Connectivity Acceptance Support is not the production-session-delivery signoff authority. Procedural guidance must not be represented as universal technical enforcement.

The executable policy owner is the versioned milestone contract. See [[concept/milestone_state_machine]].
