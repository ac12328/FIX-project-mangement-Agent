# Public Demo Wiki Guide

Read docs/PUBLIC_DEMO_POLICY.md first. The explanatory pages are newly authored drafts with low confidence and real local-source locators; they inherit no institutional verification. The JSON milestone contract is the executable owner, not the draft prose.

Search returns knowledge candidates, not project truth, approval or SMTP authentication. Only independently reviewed sources may establish operational parameters. The private internal-only project-creation page and institutional market corpus are deliberately absent. No private reference attachments or learned candidate snapshot are bundled.
