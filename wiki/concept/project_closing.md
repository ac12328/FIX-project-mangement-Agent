# Project Closing Boundary

**Independently authored demo explanation; not operational approval.**

A verified PCT-completion source must belong to this project and resolve to one of the two distinct demo support roles. Client-only claims do not qualify. PCT completion couples M8 and Completed; residual work goes to separate linked support tickets. Completion notices and delivery intents are transactional; only committed pending rows may be dispatched. Unknown send outcomes remain uncertain for manual reconciliation and must not be replayed automatically. The M7 short-form fallback is an unresolved review item, not newly validated behavior.

The executable policy owner is the versioned milestone contract. See [[concept/milestone_state_machine]].
