# Milestone State Machine

**Independently authored demo explanation; not operational approval.**

The current milestone is the highest confirmed stage entered, separate from status, ball-with and next action. New projects start at M1; confirmed current evidence may support a forward jump without invented intermediate history. Backward transitions are forbidden. Inferred progress is review-only except the narrowly verified PCT-completion service. M8 entry couples to Completed under the executable contract. A later lower-layer issue is tracked without regression.

The executable policy owner is the versioned milestone contract. See [[concept/project_closing]].
